## Cleanup interpretation

A branch was eligible for deletion only when its recorded tip was an ancestor of main, no active worktree used it, and the disposition classified it as fully merged with no unique unpreserved history. The divergent local and remote `feature/revised-domain-audit` tips both met those conditions and were deleted. Divergent `feature/stgnn-a3-ablation` refs did not meet them and were retained.

The complete decision record is `final_branch_cleanup_verification.csv`; `branch_disposition.csv` records the final action for every audited branch.
