Audit basis: `main` and `origin/main` both pointed to `88bcf3ae238f3aae712b123acfa07e4b2c5e4ad1` after `git fetch --all --prune`. The machine-readable inventory and relationship table were generated by `scripts/repository_reconciliation_audit.py` from the fetched refs.

## Ancestry summary

- The V1/V2-A development history through the repository checkpoint is already reachable from `main`. This includes the revised-domain audit, revised-domain baselines, structured revised-domain work, terminal evaluation, V2 front audits, delayed-nowcast contract, prospective harness, and raster-output checkpoint.
- `feature/controlled-predictor-augmentation` is a direct descendant of `main` with seven unique commits. It contains the controlled anthropogenic augmentation implementation and provenance needed by A3.
- `origin/feature/structured-a3-final-comparison` is a direct descendant of `main` with six unique commits. It contains the final structured A0/A3 development comparison and freeze decision. Its helper source overlaps the controlled augmentation branch by design; integration must retain the configured `MODELS` behavior from the final-comparison version.
- `origin/feature/structured-a3-evaluation` is a direct descendant of `main` with nine unique commits, but it replays some controlled-augmentation and final-comparison files. Only its evaluation-specific commits should be integrated after the canonical augmentation and development-comparison components are present.
- `feature/soil-feature-screening` diverges from the pre-checkpoint ancestor `45842f9` and contains 22 unique commits, including tracked soil tables and a Parquet artifact. It is scientifically meaningful but cannot be merged wholesale without reintroducing older files and protected/large artifacts. Its conclusion and provenance are preserved by documentation and compact A3 provenance artifacts.
- `feature/model-class-diagnostic`, `feature/neural-a0-architecture-audit`, and `feature/stgnn-a3-ablation` contain unique diagnostic work. They remain historical evidence and are not current production-development paths.
- The local and remote refs named `feature/revised-domain-audit` diverge as refs (`45842f9` locally versus `e18777f` remotely), but both tips are ancestors of `main`; neither has unmerged tip content relative to the current canonical checkpoint.

## Relationship interpretation

The `commits_ahead_of_main` and `commits_behind_main` fields in `branch_relationships.csv` are ref-specific. A branch with zero commits ahead is fully reachable from `main`, even when it is many commits behind `main`. A branch with commits ahead requires either deliberate integration, selective documentation, or historical retention; it is not safe to delete merely because a later branch contains related files.

## Integration order

The reconciliation branch is built from `main` and integrates the validated structured workflow in this order:

1. controlled predictor augmentation;
2. structured A0/A3 final comparison, resolving the known shared-helper overlap in favor of the final configured implementation;
3. evaluation-specific frozen-manifest, evaluator, and compact historical-holdout artifacts;
4. concise documentation of soil screening and historical neural/graph diagnostics.

No remote branch is deleted, rebased, force-pushed, or merged into `main` during this reconciliation pass.
