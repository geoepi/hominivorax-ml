## Interpretation boundaries

These experiments concern different estimands, methods, and evidence states.
The A6 gate failures are technical and must not be described as failures of
predictive performance. Conversely, A4 structural improvements do not satisfy
the unmet U.S. ranking guardrail. The point-level distance gradient does not
identify organism movement, transmission, or the reporting process.

The W44 candidate refresh and Q1 retrospective result are recorded separately
in the [current status](status.md). Retrospective Q1 metrics remain descriptive
because target maturity is uncertified. Residual spatial correlation remains
an open diagnostic limitation; no decision in this register makes eliminating
it a qualification criterion.
