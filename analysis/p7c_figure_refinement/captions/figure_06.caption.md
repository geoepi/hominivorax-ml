P4 support classes are shown without threshold changes. SUPPORTED indicates predictor conditions well represented in the training data; CAUTION indicates less well supported predictor conditions; EXTRAPOLATIVE indicates predictor conditions substantially outside training support. These are predictor-space support states. They are not confidence intervals, not direct measures of correctness, and not causal categories. Extrapolative predictions remain visible. The canonical P4 support display is reused because complete cell-level support records are not available for a new focal re-render.
