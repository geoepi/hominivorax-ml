## P7G-A. Spatial dependence of raw occurrence and occurrence residuals

Canonical queen-graph shell association for raw occurrence and the canonical
occurrence residual (`observed_positive - p_occurrence`) across h=1–3. Shells
are exact shortest-path distances 1–4 and summaries use pooled node means over
the eligible P2 rolling-origin records. Source: P2 prediction archive and the
canonical queen graph. This is a descriptive dependence diagnostic, not a new
spatial validation or causal analysis.

The plot is a retrospective predictive-ranking diagnostic; it is not a causal
