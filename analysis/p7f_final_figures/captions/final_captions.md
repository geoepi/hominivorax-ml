# Final P7F figure captions

## Figure 1. Domain and model framework

Canonical spatial domain, fixed Mexico-Texas focal region, frozen STRUCTURED_A3 hurdle structure, and E0_LAST_AVAILABLE operational input concept. Panels A-B use the corrected P7D boundary and shared domain-color layers; Panel D shows the latest legitimate environmental state held across h=1-3. Source phases: P0, P5A, P6, and P7D. Boundaries are cartographic orientation layers and do not change model inputs.

## Figure 2. Predictive performance across horizons

Canonical P2 occurrence performance by horizon, including Brier score, AUPRC, calibration summaries, and AUROC context. Lower Brier is better; AUPRC is prevalence-sensitive. Values are retrospective rolling-origin summaries and are not causal effects or unrestricted future forecasts.

## Figure 3. Mexico-Texas spatial-temporal occurrence dynamics

Fixed focal-region occurrence-probability surfaces for 2026-W17, W22, W27, W32, and W37 at h=1, with observed positive cells overlaid, followed by the descriptive expected-number-of-positive-cells time series. Shared spatial and color scales are used across weeks. The display is descriptive and does not imply causal spread or regional uncertainty intervals.

## Figure 4. Rolling-origin 1-3 week nowcast trajectories

Observed positive cells and repeated focal-region rolling-origin nowcasts from all 21 prespecified origins, with h=1-3 represented by point shape. Highlighted origins are W16, W20, W24, W28, W32, and W36. Panel B shows expected minus observed positive cells; Panel C shows the error distribution by horizon. No regional uncertainty ribbon is available in the canonical artifacts.

## Figure 5. Issue-time environmental proxy validation

Paired E0_LAST_AVAILABLE minus REALIZED_ENV differences for occurrence and count metrics across h=1-3, using the canonical P5A comparison. E0 uses the latest legitimately available environmental state at issue time; REALIZED_ENV uses target-week realized environmental inputs. The comparison concerns predictive outputs, not equality of environmental conditions.

## Figure 6. Predictor associations

Signed occurrence- and conditional-count-component coefficient associations with predictor families and scale classes shown. Coefficients are conditional predictive associations; magnitudes are most comparable within compatible scaling classes. Correlated predictors may redistribute coefficient magnitude, and no causal or global feature-importance interpretation is intended.
